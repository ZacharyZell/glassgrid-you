Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

GlassGrid You is designed to be strictly local and privacy-first. We do not collect telemetry, crash analytics, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or viewing history, and we do not operate backend tracking servers.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

All application state stays exclusively on your device inside standard macOS storage:

  • WebKit Session Storage: Cookies, login sessions, and site data for YouTube, YouTube TV, and any enabled streaming services or Companion Web Tiles are stored locally in Apple's WKWebsiteDataStore. You can clear all site data or reset an individual service's cookies & site data anytime from the Sidebar or GlassGrid You Settings (with clear notices where providers share single sign-on, such as Google or MyDisney).
  • App Preferences: Appearance, enabled streaming services, Clean-Box streaming player preference, Multiview presets, pinned channels, folders, Mini-Player & Grid frames/opacity, Continue Watching resume points, opt-in Live Pulse preferences (league filters, Spoiler Delay, Sleeper username, ESPN Public League IDs), and AI & Automation permission toggles are stored locally in macOS UserDefaults.
  • Optional Live Pulse 24-Hour Sleeper Player Cache: Only when you enable Live Pulse and configure a Sleeper fantasy league, GlassGrid You caches Sleeper's public player directory locally on disk for up to 24 hours at ~/Library/Application Support/GlassGridYou/SleeperCache/ (projected player ID, name, team, and position only), and lets you clear it anytime in Settings → Live Pulse.
  • Optional Loopback Bearer Token: If you explicitly enable the optional loopback HTTP MCP endpoint, its 256-bit Bearer token is generated locally and stored strictly in an owner-only (0600) file at ~/Library/Application Support/GlassGridYou/http-token—never in UserDefaults or stdio client config files.

2. Direct Third-Party Network Connections

When you use GlassGrid You, your Mac connects directly to the services you choose to visit or enable—never proxied through us:

  • Streaming Services & Sign-In Hub: YouTube, YouTube TV, Twitch, Crunchyroll, live sports, and other selected streaming sites load directly inside Apple's native WebKit engine subject to each provider's own privacy policy. GlassGrid You never intercepts, inspects, or stores your passwords.
  • Live Pulse (Optional, Strictly Off by Default): By default, Live Pulse is disabled and makes zero network requests, timers, or WebSocket connections. When you explicitly enable Live Pulse in Settings → Live Pulse (or by opening the Ticker Bar / Companion Drawer), GlassGrid You opens dedicated cookieless, ephemeral URLSession connections (never touching your WebKit cookies or sign-in sessions) directly to public read-only sports, fantasy, and prediction-market endpoints as configured: site.api.espn.com (live scoreboards/summaries), api.sleeper.app (public Sleeper usernames/leagues/matchups), lm-api-reads.fantasy.espn.com (public ESPN fantasy leagues only; private leagues require opening ESPN Fantasy in a Companion Web Tile), external-api.kalshi.com / api.elections.kalshi.com (read-only Kalshi market quotes), and gamma-api.polymarket.com / clob.polymarket.com / wss://ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com (read-only Polymarket odds). Stream-to-game matching runs 100% locally in memory using passive tab URL, title, navigator.mediaSession.metadata (title/artist/album), meta[property="og:title"], and watch-page h1 heading hints—with strict bailout on all login, SSO, MFA, and CAPTCHA pages—and your viewing history or page metadata is never transmitted to third-party endpoints.
  • SponsorBlock (Optional, Off by Default): When explicitly enabled in Settings, GlassGrid You queries the public SponsorBlock API using a k-anonymous 4-character SHA-256 hash prefix of the video ID so the full video ID is never sent.
  • Search Autocomplete (Cookieless & Toggleable): When enabled, Command Palette search suggestions use cookieless requests and can be disabled anytime in Settings.
  • App Updates: Update checks fetch the public release metadata at https://glassgridyou.com/appcast.xml (Sparkle 2) or public GitHub Releases without transmitting personal identifiers.

3. Local AI Agent, MCP, CLI & Siri Automation (Strictly Opt-In)

All AI assistant, CLI, HTTP MCP, URL trigger, and Siri automation controls ship disabled by default and never open network listeners or transmit data back to GlassGrid You:

  • Local Transports & OS Isolation: When enabled in Settings → AI & Automation, the bundled glassgrid CLI & stdio MCP server communicates exclusively over an owner-only UNIX domain socket (0600 socket inside a 0700 directory, verified per connection via getpeereid matching your macOS UID). The optional Streamable HTTP MCP server is also off by default and, when enabled, binds strictly to 127.0.0.1 with Host, Origin, 256-bit Bearer token, and 60 req/min rate limiting.
  • Permission Tiers & Permanent Blocks: Even at the highest permission tier, external tools are hard-blocked from reading session cookies or tokens, navigating to Google/SSO login, MFA, or checkout pages, executing arbitrary JavaScript, wiping site data, or disrupting WebKit fullscreen. URL scheme triggers (glassgridyou://action/...) are separately opt-in and blocked inside WebKit pages.
  • Client Data Boundary: When you invoke Siri, Shortcuts, the CLI, or a local MCP client (such as Claude, Gemini, Antigravity, or Grok), requested status metadata (sanitized page title, URL, playback state, chapters) is returned solely over your local Mac connection to the client you configured, subject to that assistant's own privacy terms.

4. Website & Payments

  • This Website (glassgridyou.com): Uses zero cookies, zero JavaScript tracking scripts, zero external fonts, and zero third-party trackers.
  • Checkout & Billing: If you purchase the signed and Apple-notarized installer, payment processing, tax/VAT collection, and receipts are handled by Polar (polar.sh) acting as Merchant of Record under Polar's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
