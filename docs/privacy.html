Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

GlassGrid You is designed to be strictly local and privacy-first. We do not collect telemetry, crash analytics, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or viewing history, and we do not operate backend tracking servers.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

All application state stays exclusively on your device inside standard macOS storage:

  • WebKit Session Storage: Cookies, login sessions, and site data for YouTube, YouTube TV, and any enabled streaming services or Companion Web Tiles are stored locally in Apple's WKWebsiteDataStore. You can clear all site data or reset an individual service's cookies & site data anytime from the Sidebar or GlassGrid You Settings (with clear notices where providers share single sign-on, such as Google or MyDisney).
  • App Preferences: Appearance, enabled streaming services, Multiview presets, pinned channels, folders, Mini-Player opacity, Continue Watching resume points, and window layout preferences are stored locally in macOS UserDefaults.

2. Direct Third-Party Network Connections

When you use GlassGrid You, your Mac connects directly to the services you choose to visit or enable—never proxied through us:

  • Streaming Services & Sign-In Hub: YouTube, YouTube TV, Twitch, live sports, and other selected streaming sites load directly inside Apple's native WebKit engine subject to each provider's own privacy policy. GlassGrid You never intercepts, inspects, or stores your passwords.
  • SponsorBlock (Optional, Off by Default): When explicitly enabled in Settings, GlassGrid You queries the public SponsorBlock API using a k-anonymous 4-character SHA-256 hash prefix of the video ID so the full video ID is never sent.
  • Search Autocomplete (Cookieless & Toggleable): When enabled, Command Palette search suggestions use cookieless requests and can be disabled anytime in Settings.
  • App Updates: Update checks fetch the public release metadata at https://glassgridyou.com/appcast.xml (Sparkle 2) or public GitHub Releases without transmitting personal identifiers.

3. Website & Payments

  • This Website (glassgridyou.com): Uses zero cookies, zero analytics scripts, zero external fonts, and zero third-party trackers.
  • Checkout & Billing: If you purchase the signed and Apple-notarized installer, payment processing, tax/VAT collection, and receipts are handled by Polar (polar.sh) acting as Merchant of Record under Polar's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

4. Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling? Reach out anytime at support@glassgridyou.com.
